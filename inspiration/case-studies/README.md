# Case Studies

The deep end of `inspiration/`. Deeper than a quick entry in `../entries/`. A case study is warranted when something is close enough to our
thesis that we need to understand why it succeeded or failed — not just that it exists.

Prefer studies that include a **failure**. Products that shipped an interaction model like ours
and then quietly removed it are the single most valuable evidence available to us, and they're
underrepresented because nobody writes case studies about their own retreats.

## Sourcing rules

- Cite the source, and mark whether it's first-party (company blog, conference talk) or
  independent. First-party performance numbers are marketing until corroborated.
- Distinguish "this shipped" from "this worked."
- If it was discontinued, find out when and, if possible, why. Note when you couldn't.

## Structure

Copy `_template.md` into `studies/` as `NN-slug.md`.

If a study started life as a quick entry in `../entries/`, leave the entry in place and add a
pointer to the study rather than deleting it — the short version stays useful for scanning.
