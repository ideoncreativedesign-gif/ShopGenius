## 2. Gacha reel + screenshot to lock your outfit — 截图选取今日穿搭

![](05-gacha-screenshot.png)

## 3. Brand receipt as the backdrop — 拟人变装

![](06-receipt.png)
![](07-receipt-fan.png)
