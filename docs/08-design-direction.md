## Visual metaphor

What should this feel like? (A personal shopper's studio? A film you're inside of? A friend's
camera roll? A well-run shop?) The metaphor should predict a hundred small decisions.

## Visual rules

Color · Typography · Layout · Shape · Imagery · Motion · AI visualization · Provenance display

## The moment that matters most

> What should the interface make the shopper feel at the moment that matters most?

Name the moment first. For ShopGenius it is probably the instant a recommendation appears and
the shopper decides whether to believe it. Everything else is in service of that.

## AI visualization

Do **not** default to robot icons, glowing gradients, sparkles, magic animations, or assistant
avatars.

Visualize what the AI is actually doing — comparing, narrowing, reconciling, ranking,
preserving a constraint, flagging uncertainty — in the product's own visual language.

| AI behavior | How we show it | How we *won't* show it |
|---|---|---|
| Narrowing from many to few | | A sparkle |
| Uncertain between two options | | Hiding one |
| Honoring a stated constraint | | A generic "AI" badge |

## Motion

Motion carries meaning in a video-native product and is a real accessibility surface. Define:
what motion communicates, what it never communicates, and the reduced-motion equivalent for
each.
