# Research

**Status:** Not started · **Owner:** —

Claude can synthesize evidence. It cannot manufacture evidence.

Every entry is labeled **Observed → Interpreted → Hypothesized**. Keep the layers separate even
when it's tedious; collapsing them is how a hunch becomes a "finding."

## Observed

Raw. Quotes, behaviors, timings, drop-off points. No conclusions.

## Interpreted

What we think the observations mean. Attribute interpretations — including ones Claude
proposed, marked as such.

## Hypothesized

What we'd want to test next. Feeds the assumptions table in `02-facts-assumptions-decisions.md`.

## Contradictions

Where the evidence disagrees with itself. Don't resolve these prematurely — they're usually
where the segmentation is hiding.

## Missing evidence

What we'd need to know but don't. This section should never be empty.
